Quiniela metrics

What this page is for: deciding whether a jornada is worth playing, and checking which numbers known before a draw really predict hits and money. Read it top down: the next ticket, how past tickets went, which metrics matter, and how the verdicts did. No metric makes the quiniela profitable: every ticket loses money on average; the metrics only compare jornadas.

Past tickets: metrics, lights, verdict and KPIs

Every official ticket: what was expected before the draw (chances, lights, verdict) and what happened (hits, net euros). Useful to see whether green lights and verdicts were followed by good results.

Which match metrics matter: v3 hits by quintile

Each table splits past ticket matches into five groups by one metric (lowest to highest) and shows how often v3 got them right. A metric matters when the hit rate rises (or falls) steadily across the groups; ups and downs mean noise. Bold: the best group.

Verdicts: the KPIs of the tickets each verdict would have played

Rule: big (play ·B) when its 1,000 € light is green; small (single or ·D) when only the ·D 12+ light is green; skip otherwise. Each row: how the tickets of that verdict did. A good rule gives the "play" rows more back per € than "skip". The lights only use earlier tickets (no look-ahead).
