Which jornada metrics matter: the tickets' KPIs by quintile

Quintiles of the official tickets (lowest to highest value of the metric), with the mean of every KPI in each: hits of the single v3+v2F ticket, share of tickets with 12+ hits (v3+v2F·D) or 13+ hits (v3+v2F·B), money back per euro spent and share of v3+v2F·B tickets paying 1,000 € or more. A metric is useful when the KPI grows (or falls) steadily across its quintiles; ups and downs mean noise.

Verdicts: the KPIs of the tickets each verdict would have played

Rule (a first version, judged here): big (play v3+v2F·B) when its light on a prize of 1,000 € or more is green; small (play the single or the v3+v2F·D ticket) when only the light on 12+ hits of v3+v2F·D is green; skip otherwise. In the past seasons every light ranks the ticket among the tickets before it only (no look-ahead).
