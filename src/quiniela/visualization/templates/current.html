This is not a prediction of our models. It is the betting market's current view of the matches not played yet: the probabilities implied by the bookmakers' odds at the last download. The predictions of our models, and whether they were right, are in predictions.html.

Where the probabilities come from. The upcoming fixtures file of football-data.co.uk publishes each match's pre-match odds. Each odd o implies a probability 1/o; the three add up to slightly more than 100% (the bookmaker's margin), so they are normalised: pk = (1/ok) / (1/o1 + 1/oX + 1/o2). The average odds across bookmakers are used, or Bet365's when the average is missing.

Current state, not final. Odds keep moving until kick-off (injuries, line-ups, money). These are the odds as downloaded ; run invoke update to refresh them.

Only matches not played yet appear here: once played, a match leaves this list and shows up in Latest results.
