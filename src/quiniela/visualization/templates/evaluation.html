Metrics

Bold: best candidate model for the metric (the market is the reference, not a candidate). ↑ higher is better · ↓ lower is better. v3+v1F·D and v3+v1F·R are not probability models but ways of playing v3: the v3 signs with doubles and triples on the ticket (up to 48 bets a matchday; v3+v1F·R may also play an official reduction), so only their hit rate (a double or triple is a hit when the result is among its signs) and prizes are shown (v3+v1F·R's prizes count the hits of its best column), and they are never bold.

Hit rate by result

For each observed result (1, X, 2), the share of its matches that each model got right. For v3+v1F·D and v3+v1F·R a double or triple is a hit for every result it covers (a triple 1X2 is a hit whether it ends 1, X or 2), on the ticket matches; elsewhere they are v3's sign. Same league and season filters as the metrics. Informative: never bold.

Contingency tables

How many matches of each observed result (rows: what really happened) got each predicted sign (columns: what the model marked). The diagonal (bold) are the hits; the share under each count is of the row (how the real results of that kind were predicted). Same league and season filters as the metrics.

Liga F (women's league)

Evaluated apart from the men's models, on the Liga F matches of the official tickets (data/women/liga_f.parquet). The Liga F model is v1F, the public's picks (share of bettors marking each sign); it is scored only on the matches whose betting page was saved (data/lae/public), a history that grows every jornada. The reference, result shares of the earlier Liga F matches (what v1F uses without picks), is scored on every played match. Season filter only.

Hit rate by confidence

Confidence: probability of the predicted sign. Matches grouped in three tiers (high, medium, low): if the confidence is reliable, the hit rate of each tier is close to its average confidence and grows from low to high. Each cell: hit rate, and the share of matches in the tier.

Official tickets by category

The 14 matches of every official ticket of the backtest seasons, by category, and how v3 did on the La Liga / Segunda ones (M) it could predict. Women's (F) and other matches (O) have no model: v3 uses the public's picks for them, which are only known for the ticket being sold, not for past ones.

Real tickets in euros

The official tickets of past draws (Loterías y Apuestas del Estado, saved in data/lae) with at least 10 La Liga / Segunda matches, played with the v3 signs (the other matches get the home sign) and paid with the real prizes of each draw, sharing each pool with the real winners. v3+v1F·E (prudent) plays only when the expected prize from 10 to 13 hits is above the cost; the full version also counts 14 hits and the Pleno al 15, rare but large prizes that a few hundred draws cannot confirm: read its net result together with the number of 13 and 14-hit tickets. The big-win mode plays every draw (at most 50 €) for the best chance of 10,000 € or more; it is judged by how many such prizes came against how many its plans expected.

Hit rate by season

Calibration

Every predicted probability (the three outcomes of every match) grouped in 10% bins, against how often the outcome happened. A calibrated model sits on the diagonal: below it, the model is over-confident in that range; above it, under-confident. All seasons of the selected league.

How to read the metrics

Every past season is predicted matchday by matchday, each model trained only on matches played before that matchday (walk-forward backtest). Notation: N matches, pik the probability given to outcome k (1, X, 2) in match i, oik = 1 if that outcome happened, else 0.
