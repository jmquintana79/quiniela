Hit rate by confidence

Confidence: probability of the predicted sign. Matches grouped in three tiers (high, medium, low): if the confidence is reliable, the hit rate of each tier is close to its average confidence and grows from low to high. Each cell: hit rate, and the share of matches in the tier.

Real tickets in euros

The official tickets of past draws (Loterías y Apuestas del Estado, saved in data/lae) with at least 10 La Liga / Segunda matches, played with the v3 signs (the other matches get the home sign) and paid with the real prizes of each draw, sharing each pool with the real winners. v5 (prudent) plays only when the expected prize from 10 to 13 hits is above the cost; the full version also counts 14 hits and the Pleno al 15, rare but large prizes that a few hundred draws cannot confirm: read its net result together with the number of 13 and 14-hit tickets.

Hit rate by season

Calibration

Every predicted probability (the three outcomes of every match) grouped in 10% bins, against how often the outcome happened. A calibrated model sits on the diagonal: below it, the model is over-confident in that range; above it, under-confident. All seasons of the selected league.

How to read the metrics

Every past season is predicted matchday by matchday, each model trained only on matches played before that matchday (walk-forward backtest). Notation: N matches, pik the probability given to outcome k (1, X, 2) in match i, oik = 1 if that outcome happened, else 0.
